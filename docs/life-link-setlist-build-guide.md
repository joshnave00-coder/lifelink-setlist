# Build Your Own Life Link Setlist App

A guide for Holli Kelly, drums on the Life Link worship team. Josh has a rehearsal player in his Muse called Life Link Setlist. It cannot be sent to another person's Muse, so this guide has everything your Muse needs to build you the same one in your own Muse. Paste the prompt in section 1 to your Muse, then answer its questions. Building it in your own Muse is the better version anyway: your markers and settings will save, which a shared copy cannot do.

## 1. The prompt to paste to your Muse

Paste this exactly, then follow up with anything it asks:

> Build me a rehearsal player app called Life Link Setlist for the Life Link Church worship team. It is set-list-first: it opens on the next service set list, shows each song in plan order with its scheduled key, and tapping a song expands it to show its audio versions, lyrics, and charts. It has prev/next set navigation and a set dropdown. The design is Life Link themed: white background, the real Life Link logo from lifelinkchurch.com, title "Worship Team", teal-to-blue gradient. Pull the set lists, keys, recordings, lyrics, and charts from Planning Center Services at services.planningcenteronline.com, signing in with my Planning Center account. The set lists to load are in section 4 of the guide Josh sent me, with plan links. For each song, download the recordings attached to the plan item (full mix plus alto, soprano, tenor, singers, and bass part tracks where they exist) and the chart PDFs attached to the plan item's Files tab. Features: play any version, play a whole set in order, follow-along lyrics overlay where tapping a line jumps to that part, tap-to-mark (tap a verse or chorus bubble while listening to set a marker; markers belong to the song and carry to another version only when its total length is within 5 seconds), pitch-shift any track to the scheduled key, and search the song library. Every song must have lyrics; check the plan item's Files tab for lyrics PDFs, not just the song record's Files panel. Do not build transposed chord charts, part blend, record-yourself, plan notes, a karaoke section, or a Stem Mixer. Build it as a server-backed app in my Muse so my markers and settings save.

## 2. What the app is

A practice tool for the worship team, not a general music player. It opens on the next service set list. Each song shows its scheduled key from the Planning Center plan. Tapping a song expands it to its tracks, lyrics, and charts. You can play one version, play the whole set in order, follow lyrics while listening, tap to mark sections, and pitch-shift any track to the scheduled key. Josh's copy holds 29 songs, 122 recordings, lyrics for 17 songs, and 43 chart PDFs.

## 3. Where the data comes from

Planning Center Services, host services.planningcenteronline.com. Your Muse signs in with your Planning Center account and opens each plan at https://services.planningcenteronline.com/plans/PLAN_ID. The plan page shows the set list in order and each song's scheduled key. The recordings and charts live on the plan items, not on the song records: open a song in the plan, go to its Files tab, and download the audio files and chart PDFs from Arrangement Attachments and Key Attachments. The song record's Files panel alone misses files, so always check the plan item's Files tab.

## 5. Features checklist

- Opens on the next service set list, with prev/next set navigation and a set dropdown.
- Songs in plan order, each showing its scheduled key.
- Tapping a song expands it to versions (full mix and part tracks), lyrics, and charts.
- Play any version; play a whole set in order.
- Follow-along lyrics overlay; tapping a lyric line jumps playback to that part.
- Tap-to-mark: tap a verse or chorus bubble while listening to set a marker there. Markers belong to the song and carry to another version only when that version's total length is within 5 seconds.
- Pitch-shift any track to the scheduled key.
- Search across the song library.
- Life Link branding: real Life Link logo, title "Worship Team", teal-to-blue gradient.

## 6. What not to build

No transposed chord charts, no part blend, no record-yourself, no plan notes in the player, no karaoke section, no Stem Mixer.

## 7. Gotchas your Muse should know

- Worthy Of It All has no downloadable audio. Use its editor-text lyrics and link its three SongSelect chart PDFs.
- Gratitude and Believe For It full mixes are large, over 17 MB each.
- A band position heading in Planning Center holds multiple people; it is one slot, not separate slots.
- The plan shows each song's scheduled key; read it there instead of asking.
- If a song has no lyrics in Planning Center, ask Holli for the words rather than leaving a gap.
- The app should keep working as new plans are added; the set-list section should let her pick a date and get that set's songs and keys.
